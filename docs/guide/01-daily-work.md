# Your daily round

**In one sentence:** clear three queues each morning, and look at three more
things each week.

**Status:** Live. Checked against the running screen on 2026-09-14.

---

## Can I break anything by doing this?

No. Everything below is either a decision that can be made again, or reading.
Nothing here deletes a document.

---

## Every morning: open Today

[Today](pages/today.md) is the screen you land on, and it is the whole round in
one page: four lists, each with its count and its button, then the failures you
can act on, then the coverage sentence and the week's report.

Work the lists in the order Today shows them. Each one feeds the next, so
clearing Review first means fewer things arrive in the others.

### 1. Review

**The number to watch:** **Documents to review** on Today, and the same count
beside **Review** in the menu.

Documents the system found but will not publish without you. This is the real
work, and it is where your time should go.

Open each row, read the one-line reason, check the PDF, approve or reject.

Full instructions: [Review](pages/review.md).

**When to stop:** when the count reads zero, or when you have given it the time
you have. An untouched Review queue costs nothing overnight — nothing is lost and
nothing expires because you did not get to it.

### 2. Missing documents

**The number to watch:** **Missing documents** on Today, and the same count in
the menu.

Items the system searched for everywhere it knows and found no document for.
Start with the oldest card: try the manufacturer's download page or the web
search it offers, and upload what you find. If you think the manufacturer has
published something since, press **Search again**.

Full instructions: [Missing documents](pages/missing.md).

The other two kinds of task the system hands to a person are not on this list:
a document that needs a decision waits on Review, and a task that failed for
good shows under Failed. The operators' [Manual](pages/manual.md) screen still
lists all three.

### 3. Failed tasks

**The numbers to watch:** the lines under *Failed tasks* on Today. A line
you cannot see is a line at zero, and the whole section disappears when nothing
has failed.

Tasks the system tried repeatedly and gave up on, in three kinds. The first two
have a button on Today itself; all three are explained on
[Failed tasks](pages/failed.md):

- **Websites that took too long.** Press **Try the N again**. It usually works.
- **Addresses that no longer work.** Press **Search again for these N**. The
  system looks for each document's new address.
- **For the developer.** Nothing to do. Retrying will not help until the cause
  is fixed.

A task you have sent back shows as *trying again* or *searching again* and stops
counting, so these three can add up to less than the **dead jobs** tile on
[System status](pages/status.md), which counts every failed task ever.

If the same website or address keeps coming back, do not keep pressing. Tell a
developer, and name the website.

Full instructions: [Failed](pages/failed.md).

## Once a week

### Expiry

Which documents are lapsing, and which have lapsed.

The system flags a document **30 days** before its date. Declarations carry no
expiry of their own, so they are instead flagged five years after issue — see
[the 5-year rule](glossary.md#the-5-year-rule).

Look at the "expiring soon" section. Anything there is a supplier to chase.

Full instructions: [Expiry](pages/expiry.md).

### Renewal emails

Renewal requests the system has written for you, based on what Expiry found.

**The system never sends them.** Read the email, edit it if you want, send it
from your own mail, then mark it *Sent* so the system stops reminding you.

Full instructions: [Renewal emails](pages/drafts-out.md).

### Emails received

What arrived, and what the system found attached. Mostly reassurance that a
supplier's reply was picked up. Nothing to decide.

Full instructions: [Emails received](pages/emails-in.md).

## When something new arrives

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| A supplier emails you a document directly | [Upload](pages/upload.md) it. One PDF at a time |
| A new product list from Business Central | [Import](pages/import.md). It shows you what will change before anything happens |
| A new supplier you have never held documents for | Add them under [Manufacturers](pages/manufacturers.md) first, then the system can start looking |
| An auditor asks where a date came from | Open the document under [Documents](pages/documents.md). Every fact shows the page it was read from |

---

## What good looks like

- Review at or near zero most days.
- No *Failed tasks* section on Today at all, or only its developer line.
- Coverage flat or rising.
- Nothing in Expiry that you have not either chased or decided to let go.

If Review keeps filling faster than you can clear it, that is worth raising. It
usually means one supplier changed how they publish, and a developer can fix it
once rather than you deciding it a hundred times.
