# Search

**In one sentence:** one box that looks across your items, documents and
suppliers at once.

**Status:** Live. Checked against the running screen on 2026-09-15.

---

## What this is

This screen is read-only. Typing into the box cannot change anything.

It searches three things in one go: your products, your documents, and your
suppliers. Instead of knowing which screen owns the answer, you paste what you
have and it shows you the first few matches from each.

---

## When you use it

- You have a product reference, a certificate number, a supplier name, or a
  document number in hand, and you do not know which screen it lives on.

---

## Before you start

Nothing. Being logged in is enough.

---

## What you do

1. Click the search box above the menu on the left, or open **Search**.
2. Type or paste what you have — an item reference, an item name, a
   supplier's article number, a certificate number, or a document number.
3. Press **Search**.

---

## What happens then

Three lists appear, each showing up to ten matches:

| Section | Searches | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| **Items** | Your own item number, the item name, or the supplier's own article number | Item no., Name, Manufacturer |
| **Documents** | The certificate number. It can also match a document's own file fingerprint, though you are unlikely to ever type one of those | Number, Type, Regulation, Cert number, Valid to, Status |
| **Manufacturers** | The supplier's name | Manufacturer, Items |

If you type a plain number, it is also checked as a document number directly,
and shown on its own above the three lists.

Each list links to a page with the complete set of matches, in case there are
more than ten.

---

## What can go wrong

| You see | It means | What to do | Good or bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nothing in one or more sections | Nothing matched that piece of text | Try a shorter piece of the number or name | Not a fault |
| Fewer than ten results | You have seen everything that matched | Nothing | Fine |
| Exactly ten results in a section | There may be more | Click the link under that section for the full list | Not a fault |

Search looks for your text anywhere inside the field, not only at the start.
It does not guess what you meant, and it does not correct spelling — if you
type an item name slightly wrong, it may find nothing. Try a shorter, exact
piece of the reference or number instead.

---

## Words the screen uses

| The screen says | It means |
|---|---|
| **Item no.** | Dentalia's own number for the product, the one printed on the article |
| **Type**, shown as *Declaration of Conformity*, *EC certificate*, *Instructions for use*, *ISO certificate* | What kind of document it is |
| The small coloured label in the **Status** column (*Published*, *Waiting for review*, *On file*, *Replaced*, *Rejected*) | See [document status](../glossary.md#document-status) |
