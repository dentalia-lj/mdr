# Discovery

**In one sentence:** what the system has gone looking for, and what nobody has
ever searched.

**Status:** Live. Checked against the code on 2026-09-03.

**This page is read-only.** It starts no searches; it only shows what has and
has not been attempted.

---

## What this is

Coverage tells you whether we hold paperwork for a product. It cannot tell you
whether anyone ever went looking. Those are different facts, and read very
differently side by side.

Checked on 2026-09-03: **4.256 of 4.265 medical-device products have never been
searched for**. Nearly everything we hold arrived from the supplier folders we
were given, not from a search.

---

## The three states

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| **Never searched** | Nothing has ever gone looking for this product's paperwork |
| **Searched, found nothing** | We looked and came back empty-handed. A genuinely different problem |
| **Searched, found something** | A search returned at least one document |

The middle state is the one worth having. Without it, "we hold nothing for this
product" and "there is nothing to be had" look identical, and only one of them
is a reason to write to the supplier.

---

## Reading a row

Rows are grouped by product family, ordered by how many medical-device products
sit in each, so the top of the list is where a search buys the most.

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Manufacturer** | The supplier. Click through to their page |
| **Group** | The family of products this row covers |
| **Device items** | How many of them BC marks as medical devices |
| **Items** | How many products in total |
| **Attempts** | How many times a search has run |
| **Last searched** | When, or **never** |

---

## What to do about it

Nothing starts from this page. Open the supplier and use **Search this supplier**
on their own page, which walks the same list in the same order. Searching is
capped per press on purpose: it makes real requests to suppliers' websites at a
polite rate.
