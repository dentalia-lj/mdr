Name the playbook

Lower-case letters, digits and single hyphens — it becomes the page address. Shorter than the full legal name is normal (ivoclar, not ivoclar-vivadent-ag).

Or take it out of the queue

Not every supplier belongs here. A distributor or a reseller issues no declarations of its own, so there is nothing to find. Say which and why — the reason is required, because months later "this supplier files nothing" and "we agreed it has nothing to file" look identical from the outside.
