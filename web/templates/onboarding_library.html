Many manufacturers publish one page that lists their declarations and instructions. Point us at it and we turn that page into every document on it, instead of searching for them one item at a time.

A probe looks and reports. It reads one page, downloads no documents, archives nothing and writes no playbook. It obeys the site's own rules: if their robots.txt says we may not read that page, it says so and stops.

The list, not one document. {% if domains %}Try {% for d in domains %}{{ d }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %} — look for “Downloads”, “Documents”, “IFU” or “Declarations”.{% endif %}

Leave this alone unless you know better.
