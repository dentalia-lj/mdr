Contacts for renewal requests

One address per line. These are who a renewal request is addressed to when a document is about to expire — the first line is used first, so put the most reliable mailbox at the top.

Role mailboxes only. An address naming a person is refused: people leave, and a name in a playbook is personal data kept in the repository. Use info@, regulatory@ or a departmental address. Do not enter the address of a notified body (TÜV, BSI, DNV) — they appear in the documents but do not issue the manufacturer's declarations.

Probe a library page

A probe looks and reports. It fetches one page, archives nothing, extracts nothing and writes no recipe. It is bound by every fetch rule the pipeline is: robots.txt is checked first and a refusal is reported rather than worked around, and it waits its turn behind the politeness interval like any other request to that host.

The page that lists the documents, not a document. A registration-gated library cannot be probed.

A regular expression matched against each link's resolved absolute URL, not the raw href.

Blank unless the documents sit somewhere other than the library page's own site. One host per line, no path — many manufacturers list on their own domain and serve the PDFs from a CDN, and without the CDN named here every one of those links is counted off-host and skipped.

A hard ceiling of {{ crawl_max_links_ceiling }}. Every link over the cap is counted and reported, never dropped silently.

One needle=TYPE per line: a casefolded substring of the URL, and one of {{ doc_types | join(", ") }}. An empty value excludes — sds_= keeps a safety-data-sheet family out of the registry while still counting it, which a negative pattern would not.
